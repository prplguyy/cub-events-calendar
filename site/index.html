CUB Events Calendar

Every campus event, club event, TOS night and sports event from the Student Clubs SharePoint, in one calendar. Each event says which club hosts it and links to its SharePoint page. New and changed events show up automatically.

On an Android phone?The Google Calendar app opens Google’s add-links and then doesn’t add anything, so add it by pasting the link instead:
  1. Tap the blue button to copy the calendar link.
  2. Press and hold this link and choose Open in new tab (a normal tap opens the app, which doesn’t work): Google Calendar → Add by URL
  3. In that tab, open the browser menu (⋮) and tick Desktop site. Paste the link into “URL of calendar” and tap Add calendar.
  4. In the Calendar app: menu → Settings → the new CUB calendar → turn on Sync.
Or do it once on a laptop; it then shows up on your phone too.
On an iPhone?Tap Add to iPhone Calendar, then “Subscribe”. It works straight from the phone. Google Calendar users: do it once on a laptop instead; the app can’t add calendars.

Apple / Outlook

Google: press “Add to Google Calendar”, then confirm. On a phone, do it once in a browser and it syncs to the app.

Outlook web or another app: copy the link and paste it into “Subscribe from web” / “Add calendar from URL”.

Calendar apps refresh subscriptions every few hours, so a brand-new event can take a while to appear.
