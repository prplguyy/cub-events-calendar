CUB Events Calendar

Every campus event, club, TOS night and sports event from the Student Clubs SharePoint, in your Google Calendar. Each club and category arrives as its own calendar in its own colour. Untick the ones you don't care about. New events show up automatically.

Add all calendars to GoogleLoading…
  1. Google Calendar opens with every calendar listed and ticked.
  2. Press Add all.
  3. Later, untick any calendar under Other calendars to hide it, or remove it in Settings.

What's included

Apple Calendar, Outlook or another app? These can only add one calendar per link, so use a single calendar with everything in it.
