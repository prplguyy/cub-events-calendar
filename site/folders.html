CUB Events · Folders

One calendar per category and per club. Add them all and tick on or off the ones you want in the “My calendars” list in Google Calendar, each with its own colour.

On an Android phone?The Google Calendar app opens Google’s add-links and then doesn’t add anything, so add it by pasting the link instead:
  1. “Add all” needs a laptop. On the phone, tap Copy next to a calendar.
  2. Open a new tab, type calendar.google.com in the address bar, open the browser menu (⋮) and tick Desktop site.
  3. Next to “Other calendars” tap + → From URL, paste, and tap Add calendar.
  4. In the Calendar app: menu → Settings → the new CUB calendar → turn on Sync.
Repeat for each calendar you want, or press “Add all” once on a laptop; they then show up on your phone too.
On an iPhone?“Add all” is for Google Calendar and works best on a laptop. To add clubs straight to the iPhone Calendar, tap Add next to each one and choose “Subscribe”.
